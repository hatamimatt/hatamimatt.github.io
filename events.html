23–29 September 2024 · Southeastern United States

Hurricane Helene

Helene made landfall in Florida's Big Bend as a major hurricane and drove catastrophic inland flooding in the southern Appalachians. ERA5 puts 369 mm (14.5 in) of rain at Asheville, North Carolina, most of it in two days.
